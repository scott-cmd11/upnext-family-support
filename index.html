Common Help

I cannot find a calendar link

Look in the source app for labels such as Subscribe, Export, iCal, Calendar Feed, or Calendar Sync. If the link opens in Apple Calendar, UpNext Family can often import it from Apple Calendar instead.

My feed stopped refreshing

Some providers rotate or expire calendar links. Copy a fresh subscribe or iCal link from the source app and add it again.

Apple Calendar import does not show a calendar

Confirm the calendar is visible in Apple Calendar and has upcoming or recent events. Then return to UpNext Family and refresh Apple Calendars.

I bought Lifetime Unlock on another device

Open Settings, choose Lifetime Unlock, and tap Restore purchase. This requires the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings and choose Delete all local data. This removes local family data from the device.
